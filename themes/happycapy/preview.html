Usage

Do's & Don'ts

Do
  • Use #f9f6f1 as the public canvas.
  • Keep black pill CTAs as the primary action.
  • Let Instrument Serif own the marketing voice.
  • Use orange only for sparse emphasis.
  • Frame media with large radius and simple borders.
  • Keep section gaps generous and editorial.
Don't
  • Do not replace the paper surface with pure white.
  • Do not use orange as the primary button fill.
  • Do not introduce blue-purple AI gradients.
  • Do not add heavy SaaS card shadows.
  • Do not make the landing page feel like a dense dashboard.
  • Do not remove the illustrated/handmade rhythm.
